How we work

Honest first

Third-party means third-party, limits are stated up front, and anything not yet verified is labeled unverified — on the store page, not in the fine print.

Privacy by default

Local-first where it fits, sync off until you turn it on, no third-party trackers and no data selling. Ever.

Evidence over claims

Health content is registered against official or peer-reviewed sources and checked against the original text before it ships.

Small, deep, finished

We would rather make one app properly than five apps thinly. Features are cut before quality is.
